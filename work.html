EcoBean

A student-led startup making plant-growing kits from recycled cans, cardboard, and newspaper, with homemade fertilizer from used coffee grounds. Built to help elementary and middle schoolers turn what they learn about climate change into real action.

  • Engineered the fertilizer through iterative testing and product optimization.
  • Led a 30-person team across marketing, finance, HR/PR, and production; sold 27 kits for $540 after a last-week prototype push in the Robotics Lab.
  • Placed 3rd in Singapore at the Junior Achievement 2025–26 competition, the first SAS team in the top three in six years.
  • Ran climate and entrepreneurship workshops for a Girl Scouts troop earning their Budding Entrepreneur badge, and for an elementary school.

Project Wish

A school service club supporting two charities that grant wishes to seriously ill patients. It started in 2024, when I put up posters around Robertson Quay for Ambulance Wish Singapore's Cycle of Peace charity race.

I started Project Wish to show students at my school how to persevere through difficult times, and that small actions can make a huge impact on people's lives.

Make-A-Wish SingaporeGrants wishes to children under 18 fighting critical illnesses.
Ambulance Wish SingaporeFulfils one final wish for adults at the end of life.“Fulfilling Wishes, Celebrating Lives”
  • One of only three clubs approved out of 50+ competitive applications.
  • Run fundraisers, build partnerships, and coordinate service opportunities for 30+ members.
  • Planned the school's first social media fundraiser; the club received the New Service Club Award from the SAS Eagle Service Council in 2026.

Code For All

An international service project that teaches underprivileged children to code, hosts workshops, and repairs old laptops to donate.

I joined without knowing how to code, and stayed because I believed in the mission. As co-president, I've used that outside view to grow the club beyond pure coding into hands-on tech and robotics.

  • Led development of a 10-week AI and vibe-coding curriculum; currently confirming its implementation. This AI work laid the foundation for LaunchLab.
  • Overseeing the Stick 'Em initiative, which brings robotics and coding together for more hands-on lessons with younger students.
  • Led digital literacy presentation development for a service conference hosted by the Jalan Besar Project.
