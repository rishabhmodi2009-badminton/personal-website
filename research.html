Why do coffee grounds help plants grow?

current research

EcoBean's coffee-ground fertilizer worked in testing, but I wanted to know why. I'm now turning that question into a British Science Association Gold CREST Award project.

  1. 2025 · EcoBeanThe first testMixing grounds straight into soil grew mold, so we switched to a coffee-ground "tea" fertilizer. Seedlings fed on day 4 grew far faster by day 5 than a water-only control.
  2. May 2026 · AP Chemistry finalThe chemistryWith my partner Liam, I traced the effect to nutrients, pH, equilibrium, and decomposition kinetics.
  3. Now · Gold CREST AwardThe full project70+ hours of research, testing, and a written report, assessed against the CREST Gold criteria.
